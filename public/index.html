Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking with Haris Travel Agency.

How do I get an exact travel quote?

You can fill out the form below or message us directly on WhatsApp with your travel destination, preferred dates, and number of passengers. We reply with detailed flight options, hotels, and fares within minutes.

Can you arrange visa assistance as well?

Yes! We provide complete visa guidance and document preparation for Dubai, Malaysia, Turkey, UK, Canada, Italy, Schengen states, USA, and Saudi Umrah visas.

Do you make custom packages for families & couples?

Absolutely. Whether it is a honeymoon, a relaxed family vacation with children, or an executive business trip, we tailor every day of the itinerary according to your schedule and budget.

Do you offer customized Umrah packages?

Yes. We offer both economy and VIP 5-star packages near the Haram in Makkah and Madinah, including private luxury transport, ziyarat, and complete ritual guidance.

Will someone assist me during the trip?

Yes. Our WhatsApp support remains active throughout your entire travel period until you return safely back to Karachi.
